# Gilman News Project

App for local school newspaper.

Currently has ability to interface with online database to display articles, images, and authors. Advanced Search Functionality has been implemented. User Stats have been implemented and store information on local device for simplicity. Recommended authors and following of specific author currently implemented. Read/unread articles implemented. roughly 3-4 years of articles as well as 30 years of handpicked archives (so 530) available currently. Two Games currently available - Curling (lol) and Gilman Connections. If interested in Beta Testing, reach out here: gilmannews@gilman.edu and I will get back to you. If you would like to use this code for your own newspaper, please contact me and I will help you port this over.

Most Recent Update 8/31/26 - 1.1.3 Update: Refactored the article cards, most menues. Standardized app bars on the top to look the same. Added cross platform support for Ipads (Macs and Laptops are likely soon to follow). As for actual content added 2 new pages. The Article of the day page as well as the Archive section. This coincided with the publishing of ~50 handpicked archived articles from the past 75 years. Plenty of other ui fixes and bug fixing.
